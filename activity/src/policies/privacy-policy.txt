4. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing
Under the GDPR, personal data must be processed using a valid legal basis.

Observer may rely on the following legal bases under Article 6 GDPR.
A. Performance of a Service — Article 6(1)(b)
Processing may be necessary to provide features requested by a user or server administrator.
This may include:
● Executing bot commands.
● Providing reminders.
● Maintaining user-configured settings.
● Providing tickets.
● Processing requested message quotes.
● Providing other explicitly requested Observer functionality.
B. Legitimate Interests — Article 6(1)(f)
Observer may process personal data where necessary for legitimate interests, provided those
interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.
These legitimate interests may include:
● Operating and maintaining Observer.
● Preventing abuse and misuse.
● Protecting Discord communities from spam, scams, phishing, or malicious activity.
● Providing moderation and server-management functionality.
● Maintaining server configuration.
● Investigating errors or security incidents.
● Improving the reliability and security of Observer.
Where legitimate interests are relied upon, the processing is limited to what is reasonably
necessary for those purposes.
C. Legal Obligation — Article 6(1)(c)
Personal data may be processed where necessary to comply with a legal obligation under
applicable EU or Slovenian law.
D. Consent — Article 6(1)(a)
Certain optional features may rely on your consent where appropriate.
Where consent is the legal basis, you may withdraw that consent at any time.
Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before
consent was withdrawn.
Observer does not rely on consent where processing is necessary for a core feature and
consent could not genuinely be withdrawn.

5. Data Minimisation
Observer is designed to process only personal data reasonably necessary for the feature being
used.
Where possible:
● Discord IDs are preferred over unnecessary personal information.
● Data is not collected solely because it is available through Discord.
● Message content is not permanently stored unless a feature requires persistent storage.
● Data unrelated to Observer's operation is not intentionally collected.
6. Storage and Data Retention
Observer retains personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for
which it was collected.
Retention depends on the type of data and feature involved.
Examples include:
● Server configuration may be retained while Observer remains configured for that server.
● Leveling data may be retained until deleted, reset, or no longer required.
● Reminder data may be retained until the reminder is completed, cancelled, or removed.
● Moderation records may be retained while needed for moderation history.
● Ticket or report information may be retained while needed by the relevant server's
moderation or support system.
● Temporary message-processing data may be discarded once the relevant operation is
completed.
Where no fixed retention period is specified, retention is determined based on:
● Whether the data remains necessary for the feature.
● Whether the server continues to use Observer.
● Whether the information is required for security or abuse prevention.
● Whether retention is required by law.
● Whether a valid deletion request has been received.
Data that is no longer necessary should be deleted or anonymised where reasonably
practicable.
